Who We Are

Not a taxi.
A travel experience.

Velora Travels is a premium chauffeur-driven travel company built around one obsession: making the journey as memorable as the destination. Our fleet currently features a single, immaculately maintained flagship — the Force Urbania — looked after like a private aircraft.

We keep our fleet deliberately small so every guest receives the same standard of care: a professionally trained chauffeur, spotless interiors, punctual pickups and pricing agreed before you ever step in.

  • Fleet of one, standard of many

    Every vehicle we add must match the Urbania's benchmark — no exceptions.
  • Chauffeurs, not drivers

    Uniformed, route-trained and briefed on your itinerary before departure.
  • Designed to grow with you

    Our platform is built to add new vehicles — and new ways to travel — quickly.
